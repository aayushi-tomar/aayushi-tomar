# 👋 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science student and aspiring Backend / AI Engineer** interested in building the infrastructure that makes modern software and AI systems actually work.

My primary interests sit at the intersection of:

* ⚙️ **Backend Engineering**
* 🧠 **Generative AI & LLMs**
* 🔎 **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**
* 🏗️ **AI Infrastructure**
* 🌐 **Distributed Systems**
* 🔌 **API & Service Architecture**
* 🗄️ **Databases & Vector Search**
* 🚀 **Productionizing AI Systems**

I enjoy going beyond simply calling an LLM API.

I'm interested in understanding the **systems underneath AI applications** — how information is ingested, indexed, retrieved, processed, cached, served and monitored.

> **My goal:** build reliable backend systems and AI infrastructure that can move from a prototype to production.
